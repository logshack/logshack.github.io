Radios compatible with LogShack

LogShack for desktop controls your radio through Hamlib: frequency, mode, VFO, split and volume. Hamlib knows hundreds of radios, but each model behaves in its own way, and we can only say "it works" for the ones we have really tried.

Tested means someone connected it to LogShack and checked the functions below. Radios without the badge are in the program's menu and Hamlib supports them, but nobody has tried them yet: they probably work, with no guarantee. Other Hamlib radios can be used by typing the model number by hand in the settings.

Hai una di queste radio? Aiutaci a provarla

Bastano quattro controlli, con la radio collegata a LogShack:

  1. Frequenza e modo: compaiono nella striscia della radio e seguono la manopola.
  2. Dal programma alla radio: scrivi una frequenza o scegli un modo in LogShack e la radio li segue.
  3. Split: attiva lo split sulla radio (per esempio +4 kHz): in LogShack compare lo split e il QSO va a log con la frequenza di trasmissione.
  4. Volume: compare il cursore del volume e cambia il volume della radio. Se non compare, la radio non lo supporta via Hamlib: non è un difetto.

Scrivici il modello della radio, il sistema (Mac o Windows) e cosa ha funzionato e cosa no: metanoiastudio.dev@gmail.com. La aggiungiamo alla lista come provata.

Do you own one of these radios? Help us test it

Four checks are enough, with the radio connected to LogShack:

  1. Frequency and mode: they show up in the radio strip and follow the tuning knob.
  2. From the program to the radio: type a frequency or pick a mode in LogShack and the radio follows.
  3. Split: turn split on the radio (for example +4 kHz): LogShack shows the split and the QSO is logged with the transmit frequency.
  4. Volume: the volume slider appears and changes the radio's volume. If it doesn't appear, the radio doesn't support it through Hamlib: that's not a bug.

Send us the radio model, the system (Mac or Windows) and what worked and what didn't: metanoiastudio.dev@gmail.com. We'll add it to the list as tested.
